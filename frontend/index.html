ABOUT ACET

A Legacy of Quality Engineering & Innovation

Founded in 1998 by the Anwer Educational Trust, Anwer College of Engineering and Technology (ACET) has grown from a humble institution into an autonomous academic powerhouse. Affiliated with Anna University and accredited by NAAC with an 'A' Grade, ACET is committed to bridging the gap between rigorous technical fundamentals and industry demand.

🎯

Our Vision

To be universally recognised as a benchmark institution producing ethically grounded, technically agile engineers and entrepreneurs who solve global real-world challenges.

🚀

Our Mission

Deliver experiential project-based pedagogy, maintain industry-synced advanced laboratories, and nurture an environment of curiosity, multidisciplinary research, and lifelong learning.

🏅

Autonomous Autonomy

Conferred autonomous status by UGC & Anna University since 2018. Enables contemporary industry-oriented syllabus revisions, continuous internal evaluations, and modern electives.

🔬

R&D & Incubation

Dedicated MSME-recognised innovation hub, 8 sponsored research grants, and 5 Indian patents published across sustainable construction, low-power VLSI, and intelligent autonomous systems.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Queries About Admissions & Campus

Candidates can choose Anwer College of Engineering and Technology (TNEA Counseling Code: 1422) during the Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions single-window counseling. Our admission desk is open 6 days a week to guide students through the choice-filling procedure.

Hostel accommodation is optional but highly recommended for students traveling from outside Kanchipuram and Chennai. We provide furnished rooms (2-sharing and 3-sharing), solar water heating, high-speed Wi-Fi, and nutritious vegetarian/non-vegetarian dining.

ACET offers up to 75% tuition fee waiver for students scoring 95%+ in their 12th board exams. Additionally, Government First Graduate scholarships, Post-Matric SC/ST grants, and sports excellence scholarships are actively facilitated.

Diploma holders in Engineering or B.Sc. degree holders with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories) are eligible for direct admission into the 3rd semester across all engineering departments.
